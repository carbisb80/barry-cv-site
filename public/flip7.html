Scoring help +

Use “Enter total” for a score you’ve already calculated. That number includes any modifiers. Tick “Add Flip 7 bonus” only if your total does not yet include the extra 15.

Or choose “Card calculator”: select your distinct number cards, any + modifiers and ×2. Only number cards are doubled. Seven selected number cards automatically add 15 points. A bust scores zero.

When a completed round puts someone at 200 or more, the highest total wins. If the lead is tied, everyone plays another round.
